# 06 — no coming back once dead (#179)

Status: ✅ done — 2026-10-05
Type: feature

Decided 2026-10-05: opt-in `closed_once_active: true`, off by default.
Once the zone has activated, a human in the zone who was not among the players recorded at activation is warned, then put through the escalation that already exists for a player outside the zone: flak, then destruction past the delay.
One punishment mechanism, one delay setting (`max_seconds_outside_players`, now readable from YAML too).
