# 04 — Target coordinates: DDM and MGRS, never DMS

Status: 🔄 in progress

David's rule: a target's precise coordinates are written as DD°MM.mmm' **and** as MGRS, never DD°MM'SS.ss". The kneeboard printed DMS only; the deck and PDF target sheets printed DMS and DDM, no MGRS. And (2026-10-30) a target made of vehicles is given by the coordinates of its zone, not of a vehicle.

Done when: every place the briefing locates a target uses DDM + MGRS, tested against reference values (a known MGRS), and vehicle targets carry their zone's position.
