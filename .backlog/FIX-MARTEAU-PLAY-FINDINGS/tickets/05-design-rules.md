# 05 — Design rules in the guide and the objective prompt

Status: 🔄 in progress

Four rules learnt on Marteau du Golan, each with its reason, in `describe_authoring_guide` and the objective-mission prompt (FR and EN):

- SEAD and strike never go to the same flight.
- Little turbulence and a moderate wind whenever helicopters fly.
- A QRA with `react_on_helicopters: false` does not take off for helicopters, but once airborne it attacks them; the briefing never says otherwise, and the design plans cover.
- The QRA and the long-range SAMs can be switched off on demand, depending on who shows up: checked against the Mission Maker and Opposition menus, and documented.
