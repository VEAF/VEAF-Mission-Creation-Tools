# 05 — A zone entry is never cut between two pages

Status: ✅ done

The zone list could put a zone's MGRS and bullseye at the top of the next page, away from its title. A block that fits on a page now starts a new page rather than being cut; a block taller than a page still runs on.

Done when: on a multi-page zone list each page opens on a zone title and closes on a whole entry.
