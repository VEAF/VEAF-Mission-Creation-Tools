# FIX-RADIO-PRESET-CAPACITY — Never write more presets than a radio holds

Status: ⬜ ready

David, 2026-11-10: the OH-58D Kiowa crashes the DCS client at spawn, in multiplayer, on the Caucasus, Syria, GermanyCW and Marianas Open Trainings; it works on Normandy, Sinai and PersianGulf.
The crash is native, during the cockpit devices' initialization: the log stops right after the LMFD lua state, with no crash report.

## Diagnosis (David's, from the 2026-11-10 builds)

The dynamic-slot Kiowa templates (`OH-58 Template #01`, `OH-58 Template Red #01`) differ only by the preset count on `Radio[3]` and `Radio[4]`, the two FM sets VFM1 and VFM2:

| Mission | FM presets | Kiowa |
|---|---|---|
| Caucasus, Syria, GermanyCW, Marianas | 32 | crashes |
| Sinai, PersianGulf | 21 | works |
| Normandy | DCS defaults (no Kiowa template) | works |

The count comes from the OT's `src/presets.yaml`, `channel_lists.<coalition>.fm_supplement`: 30 entries in Syria (channels 30 to 59), 20 in Sinai (generated by its `tools/gen_12_presets.py`).
**Hypothesis:** the Kiowa's FM radios do not take more than about 20 presets plus the head slots; 21 works, 32 crashes. The exact limit is unknown.
David validates it first on Syria, in game, solo — outside this lot (see *Order of work*).

## What VMCT does today (read 2026-11-10)

- `presets_injector/data/dcs-radio-layouts.yaml` already has a `capacity: <int>` primitive per radio, applied last by `_truncate_to_capacity` (`presets_manager.py` around line 1507), which drops the excess from the end and renumbers.
- **No radio declares it.** The OH58D entry (radios 1–4) gives reserved head slots (`M` on UHF/VHF; `C` then `M` on FM, fed by entries #01 and #20) and no capacity, so a 30-entry `fm_supplement` goes through whole.
- The truncation is **silent by design** (ADR 0010, exploration doc §8.4): a debug line only, `validate` being the verbose path.

## Decisions — 2026-11-10

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Where the limit lives | `capacity` on the radio in `dcs-radio-layouts.yaml`, per aircraft type and per radio — the primitive exists, no new mechanism |
| D2 | OH-58D FM (radios 3 and 4) | `capacity: 21` (20 + the head slot, David's "20 + Guard"): the count that works on Sinai. Re-read in the module files (`C:\jeux\DCS World\Mods\aircraft\OH58D\`) whether the real count is documented, and say what was found; without a source, 21 stays and the entry says it is measured, not documented |
| D3 | The warning | A truncation becomes a **warning at build**, naming the unit type, the radio, the capacity and how many entries were dropped — David asked for it explicitly, and a dropped preset is one a pilot will look for. This reverses the "silent by design" of ADR 0010 for capacity: amend the ADR. It costs no noise today, since no radio declares a capacity — check it on the existing layouts and the shipped defaults (no warning on a default build) |
| D4 | Other types at risk | Look for a documented preset count in the module files of the other types `dcs-radio-layouts.yaml` lists (and the OH-58D's UHF/VHF radios 1–2); declare a capacity only where it is documented or measured, and list what was looked at in the Outcome. No guessed capacity |
| D5 | Scope | Do not touch the channels, the other aircraft's presets, nor the CH-47's own `radio_fm_30` list |
